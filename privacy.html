Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

CastMate: TV Remote & Hub (“CastMate”) is a 100% free, ad-free, local-first utility application. We do not collect, store, track, or share your personal information.

1. Zero Data Collection & Zero Tracking

  • No Accounts: CastMate requires no sign-up, login, or cloud account.
  • No Ads or Analytics: CastMate contains zero advertising SDKs, zero analytics trackers, and never sells or shares data with third parties.
  • 100% Local Network Operation: All Smart TV commands, media streams, Bluetooth LE scans, and smart home controls stay strictly on your device and local Wi-Fi/Bluetooth network.

2. Device Permissions Explained

  • Local Network & Bonjour: Used solely to discover and communicate with compatible Smart TVs (SSDP, mDNS, Roku ECP, LG webOS, Samsung Tizen) and local Wi-Fi smart devices (Shelly, Tasmota, Gree, Xiaomi miIO) on your home LAN. Pairing tokens are saved only locally on your device.
  • Photo Library: Accessed only when you pick a photo or video to cast. Selected files are streamed over an ephemeral local HTTP server directly to your TV on the same Wi-Fi network and are never uploaded to external servers.
  • Bluetooth Low Energy: Used only when scanning nearby BLE peripherals or reading live vehicle ECU diagnostics from a compatible BLE ELM327 OBD-II adapter.
  • Motion Sensors (CoreMotion): Used only while Air Mouse mode is enabled to translate phone rotation into relative TV pointer movement in memory. Motion data is never recorded or transmitted off your LAN.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Locally stored TV pairing tokens and smart-home IP addresses remain exclusively on your device and are permanently deleted when you forget a device inside the app or uninstall CastMate.
