CastMate Support & FAQ

Need help pairing your Smart TV, Air Mouse, or Smart Hub devices? Check the quick steps below or email us directly.

Quick Setup

  1. Make sure your iPhone/iPad and Smart TV are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open CastMate, tap Tap to Scan & Pair Smart TV, and allow Local Network access when prompted by iOS.
  3. Select your TV from the scan list, or tap Add TV by IP to enter your TV’s local IPv4 address manually.

TV & Device Tips

  • LG webOS TVs: Enable LG Connect Apps in TV Network Settings and press Accept on your TV prompt. Supports native Gyroscope Air Mouse cursor and Installed TV Apps.
  • Samsung Tizen TVs: Press Allow on your TV screen when pairing. Use Cursor mode inside Samsung Internet Browser, or switch the Touchpad pill to D-Pad to steer Home/Netflix/YouTube menus.
  • Roku TVs: Enable Settings → System → Advanced system settings → Control by mobile apps. Supports Installed TV Apps and D-Pad Steer.
  • Smart Hub (Car OBD-II, Smart Plugs & Robot Vacuum): Connect any BLE ELM327 adapter under Smart Hub → Car & BT; scan or enter IP for local Shelly/Tasmota smart plugs under Power & AC; scan UDP :54321 or enter IP + 32-hex miIO token under Xiaomi Vac.
